ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

The details behind the glow.

Coverage

The main gallery includes subtle coatings and recolours for comparison. It excludes “Hide Weapon Enchant”. A legacy recipe index preserves older names without assuming that similar colours mean identical effects.

Pictures & tags

Images are stored locally. Source links open the original wiki file page or the exact video timestamp. No recoloured mock-ups or duplicate crops are counted as extra pictures.
